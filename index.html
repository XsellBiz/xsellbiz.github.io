Frequently Asked Questions

Who is XsellBiz for?

Manufacturing and operations teams that want to reduce manual KPI reporting and establish a consistent, repeatable reporting process.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

No. XsellBiz works with agreed exports from your existing operational systems, such as Excel-based reports.

What type of reports do we receive?

Structured Excel KPI reports with agreed calculation definitions, reporting periods and applicable technical exception information.

Who is responsible for data quality?

The client remains responsible for the accuracy, completeness and business validity of the source data. XsellBiz performs agreed technical validation checks and reports identified processing exceptions.

Does XsellBiz interpret business performance?

No. Business interpretation, KPI targets and operational decisions remain with the client's management team.

Is this a one-time project or an ongoing service?

XsellBiz is primarily an ongoing KPI reporting service, with an initial implementation followed by a recurring monthly service agreement.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on the agreed reporting scope, including datasets, sites, KPI requirements, processing frequency and support needs.
